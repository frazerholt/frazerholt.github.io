About Frazer

My Life and Work

An interdisciplinary artist, poet and writer whose work explores the stranger aspects of human existence. Working across poetry, weird fiction and neo-expressionist visual art, I draw on cosmic horror, existentialism and the surreal to develop a visual and literary language that is distinctly my own. My work is often deliberately ambiguous, favouring unusual perspectives and unconventional expression over immediate interpretation.

Influences: Thomas Ligotti, H.P. Lovecraft, Cormac McCarthy, Junji Ito, H.R. Giger, Zdzisław Beksiński, Francis Bacon and MF DOOM.
