About Frazer

My Life and Work

I was born a second twin in Perth, Western Australia, in 1997, before moving to Melbourne to purse my interests in art and literature.

I'm an interdisciplinary artist, poet and writer whose work explores the stranger aspects of the human condition. I work across poetry, weird fiction and neo-expressionist visual art, drawing on cosmic horror, existentialism and the surreal to develop a visual and literary language that is distinctly my own. My work is often deliberately ambiguous, as I have a personal favour towards unusual perspectives, ambiguity and unconventional expression over immediate interpretation.
